## Overall Impression

Setup spine and Obsidian Gold discipline are strong. Biggest opportunity: **one quiet meta line under the hero**, dense movers list, kill tracked eyebrow, fix a11y on dynamic P&L and icon buttons.

## Priority Issues

### P0 — Color/dynamic status without live region; weak names on icon controls
**Why:** Screen-reader and keyboard users miss P&L updates; ×/+ rely on title only.
**Fix:** `aria-live="polite"` on summary; accessible names on remove/add; focus-visible on watchlist controls.
**Suggested command:** `$impeccable harden popup`

### P1 — Chrome vs Utmost Restraint
**Why:** Always-on footnote + privacy + separate status card + carded movers turn a glance tool into a mini dashboard.
**Fix:** Collapse methodology into “How it works”; single meta line (Live · 3m ago · Strip on); movers as hairline list not mini-cards.
**Suggested command:** `$impeccable quieter popup` / `$impeccable distill popup`

### P1 — Tracked uppercase Watchlist eyebrow
**Why:** PRODUCT anti-reference (tiny uppercase tracked kicker).
**Fix:** Match movers label pattern: “Watchlist” sentence case, normal tracking.
**Suggested command:** `$impeccable typeset popup`

### P2 — False affordance on movers; 5-min promise gap
**Why:** Hover scale implies click; product promises 5-min window but popup shows day only while footnote mentions 5-min.
**Fix:** Remove mover hover theater or open detail; show 5-min aggregate or drop footnote claim.
**Suggested command:** `$impeccable clarify popup` / `$impeccable layout popup`

### P3 — Empty emoji residue; v0.2 badge noise
**Why:** Slightly marketing/empty-app tone vs precise tool.
**Fix:** Quieter empty copy; drop version or move to Settings only.
**Suggested command:** `$impeccable quieter popup`
